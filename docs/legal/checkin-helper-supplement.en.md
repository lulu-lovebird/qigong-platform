## Purpose and eligibility

Check-ins, reflection sharing, achievement boards and honorary badges encourage sustained practice among Baiyin learners. This is a community service, not a private diary or a medical diagnosis or treatment service.

Read and explicitly accept this notice before using ordinary learner features, including after material updates. Acceptance does not approve learner eligibility; authorized Baiyin staff must still verify it. You may read policies and contact the privacy representative without accepting or while ineligible, and retain applicable data rights.

## Data and default sharing

Necessary information may include platform identification, enrollment and region information, practice dates, timezones, methods, consent versions, and security and audit records. Enrollment and contact details are not included in the learner feed.

New check-ins created after acceptance share their date, methods, reflection and feeling selections by default with eligible Check-in Helper learners and authorized staff across regions. This is not publication to the general internet. Derived totals, streaks, rankings and badges appear within the scope stated by each feature.

Use nicknames or masked labels rather than automatically publishing legal names, email addresses, phone numbers or platform identity IDs. Familiar readers may nevertheless recognize an author from content or practice information; complete anonymity is not guaranteed. Do not disclose another person's data without appropriate permission or impersonate someone else.

Reflections and feelings are optional. Avoid medical records, diagnoses, contact details or unnecessary sensitive information. If voluntarily providing health-related reflections or feelings, separately and explicitly consent to processing and sharing such information for these service purposes; ordinary acceptance alone is not sensitive-information consent.

Corrections to new shared entries update their learner-visible content. The check-in page must continue to show the sharing scope. Editing a withdrawn entry does not automatically republish it; republishing requires confirmation.

Acceptance does not publish previously private entries, and editing an old private entry does not publish it. Sharing old content requires separate preview and confirmation.

Acceptance does not authorize general public access, advertising, sale of personal data, LLM/AI training or arbitrary partner-system access. External sharing requires separate permission and credentials. WhatsApp decision-notification consent and STOP withdrawal remain separate.

## Suspension and removal of eligibility

Appropriately scoped administrators may suspend eligibility following a course pause, withdrawal or loss of qualification. Suspension is not deletion of the account or all historical data, and must not automatically affect independent applications on other platforms or administrator grants.

Suspended learners cannot read others' content, check in or publish new content. Existing links and direct API calls are also restricted. Their historical shares are hidden from other learners; previously viewed, copied or captured content cannot be recalled.

Authorized staff may retain access to history within their permissions, necessary purposes and retention rules. Staff access to shared history does not turn old private reflections into globally readable content; existing role and regional restrictions still apply to private entries.

## Rights, retention and security

Retention follows the website policy, necessary service purposes and applicable law. This supplement does not permit indefinite retention or remove applicable correction, withdrawal, restriction or deletion rights. Suspending eligibility differs from requesting account/data deletion. Deletion requests follow the original policy's applicable deadlines and exceptions, including its 30-day deletion provision where applicable.

Contact the privacy representative for requests; identity verification may be required. Explain necessary record, audit or backup exceptions and their handling. Staff access to history is not a blanket reason to refuse deletion requests.

This deployment uses a DigitalOcean server in Singapore and Cloudflare proxy services. Enabled chatbot platforms also process relevant information. Describe backup and other processor arrangements according to actual operations rather than importing unrelated website analytics or advertising purposes.

Identity verification, scoped access and appropriate safeguards are used, but zero risk or prevention of copying by authorized readers cannot be guaranteed. Use community content only for the stated purposes; do not redistribute it externally without appropriate author permission.
